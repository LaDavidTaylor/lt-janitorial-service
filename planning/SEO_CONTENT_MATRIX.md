### Overlap Rules

1. The category page explains the full problem and helps a buyer choose a direction.

2. The bookable page explains one method, room type, or scheduling intent in depth.

3. Warehouse Cleaning is the category. Warehouse Cleaning Services is the combined working package.

4. Medical Facility Cleaning is the category. Medical Facility Sanitation is the planned sanitation scope.

5. One and Done Cleaning is a broad single visit reset. One Time Project Cleaning is a custom project centered on one specialized outcome.

6. Post Construction Cleaning is the project category. Debris and Dust Removal is the broad soil phase. Final Site Cleaning is the presentation phase. Post Renovation Cleanup is for retained or occupied commercial environments.

7. No service page should borrow the first paragraph, FAQ wording, process labels, or hero promise from another service page.

5. Use `FAQPage` only when the visible questions and answers exactly match the structured data.

6. Use `BreadcrumbList` on service, industry, and service area details.

7. Use `Review` or `AggregateRating` only if implementation accurately reflects the live Google evidence and does not copy unpublished or edited review text.

8. Do not add an unsupported price range, opening hours, street address, certification, employee count, guarantee, or response time.

## Internal Linking Rules

1. Every service category links to all of its bookable children.

2. Every bookable service links back to the category and to two adjacent service intents.

3. Each city links to its verified county, three locally relevant services, and one nearby confirmed city where useful.

4. Each county links to its owner listed cities. Dallas County links to Dallas, Garland, and Mesquite. Collin County links to Plano, McKinney, and Frisco. Rockwall County links to Rockwall, Royse City, and Heath.

5. Each industry links to the two or three services that match its operating needs.

6. Service area pages should not imply proximity through a hidden or invented street address.

7. The quote page and facility walk through action should be reachable from every detail template.

## Originality and Quality Controls

1. Each city has a different local demand profile, facility mix, page hero, overview, local context, service priorities, FAQ set, metadata, and image description.

2. City pages must not be generated by replacing a place name inside a shared paragraph.

3. County pages summarize a regional mix and link into confirmed city evidence. They should not repeat the corresponding city hero.

4. Service pages separate method, room, timing, or buyer intent. They should not reuse a category page as a longer synonym.

5. Claims should remain concrete and operational. Avoid empty language such as best in Dallas, unmatched quality, guaranteed spotless, or hospital grade.

6. Images are representative commercial visuals. Alt text must describe the visible facility and must never imply that a generated image is a completed LT Janitorial project.

7. Titles target one primary intent. Descriptions should explain facility type, service, and territory without stuffing city lists.

8. Before production, run a repeated sentence scan across all service and location data. Identical navigation labels and factual disclaimers are acceptable. Marketing paragraphs should remain original.
