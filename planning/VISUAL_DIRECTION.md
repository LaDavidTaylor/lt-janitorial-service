# LT Janitorial Service Visual Direction

## Creative position

The site should feel like a premium facilities partner, not a residential cleaning company and not a generic stock photo catalog. The visual world is quiet, exact, mature, and operational. Refined architecture supports the work without overpowering it. Every scene shows believable commercial materials, equipment, posture, personal protective equipment, and cleaning methods.

The existing logo remains the source of truth. Its interlocking rounded square emblem, gold treatment, serif wordmark, and black presentation are preserved. The new website should elevate that identity through disciplined spacing, strong contrast, generous editorial photography, and restrained motion.

## Color direction

1. Primary black: `#080808`

2. Structural charcoal: `#151515`

3. Soft charcoal: `#24231F`

4. Warm ivory: `#F4F0E7`

5. Deep brand gold: `#A27638`

6. Mid brand gold: `#B89552`

7. Light brand gold: `#D4BD73`

8. Highlight gold: `#EFE291`

Gold should appear as a controlled accent, not as a large flat background. Use it for the logo, fine rules, small labels, selected icons, button emphasis, and subtle light. Warm ivory carries body text and bright surfaces. Black and charcoal create the premium foundation.

## Typography direction

Use a refined serif for display headlines that feels compatible with the existing wordmark. Cormorant Garamond, Libre Baskerville, or Georgia are suitable directions. Use a highly legible modern sans serif for navigation, body copy, forms, and labels. Inter, Manrope, or a strong system sans serif are suitable directions.

Display headlines should be large, composed, and compact. Body text should remain practical and direct. Avoid decorative scripts, condensed novelty fonts, and exaggerated luxury styling.

## Composition guidance

Hero text should sit over the deliberate dark negative space rather than over faces or equipment. Preserve the full image on wide screens. On narrow screens, use controlled object position so the worker and primary equipment remain visible. Do not crop the main hands, face, or cleaning tool.

Use modest dark overlays when copy contrast requires them. Avoid heavy gold color grading. The generated set already carries the brand palette through architecture and light.

## Replacement policy

Replace illustrative images with genuine project photography only when the client provides clear permission and confirms the property may be shown. Retain the same calm composition, commercial focus, and material palette so the site remains coherent as real photography is added.
